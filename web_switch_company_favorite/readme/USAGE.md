To switch between companies using the standard checkboxes, open the
company switcher menu and use the **Companies** tab:

![Companies Tab](static/description/tab_companies.png)

To manage and apply favorite filters, switch to the **Favorites** tab:

![Favorites Tab](static/description/tab_favorites.png)

From the **Favorites** tab you can:

- **Apply a filter**: Click on a filter name to apply its company
  combination.
- **Create a filter**: Click on the **Create** button. A dialog opens
  where you can name the filter, if you remove all users the filter will
  be *Shared* to all users (on desktop screens only).
- **Edit a filter**: Click on the gear icon next to the filter (on
  desktop screens only) to open the edit dialog.
- **Delete a filter**: Click on the trash icon next to the filter (on
  desktop screens) to delete it.

Your private favorite filters are displayed at the top, separated from
the shared filters by a horizontal line.
