This Odoo module allows you to manage company representatives and their
representative roles.

A representative is a contact (res.partner) assigned to a specific company
and representative role. This relationship is managed separately from the
partner's parent contact, as a contact being associated with a
company does not necessarily mean that they represent the company.

The structured representative data can be used by other modules, for example
to display representatives and their roles in external reports or documents.

Features:
- Assign contacts as representatives to companies.
- Assign a representative role to each representative.
- Manage representative roles centrally.
- Define the display order of representatives.
- Define representative sequences per company.
- Prevent duplicate representatives for the same company.
- Display representatives and their roles on the company form.
- Allow the same contact to represent multiple companies independently.
