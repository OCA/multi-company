Bridge module that exposes the ``Own Company`` field of
``multi_company_field_visible`` on the **contact** form for users
without multi-company access. It is auto-installed when both
``multi_company_field_visible`` and ``partner_multi_company`` are
present.

.. contents::
   :local:

Configuration
=============

Go to *Settings > General Settings > Companies* and toggle **Own company
on contacts** to show or hide the field on contacts. It is enabled by
default.
